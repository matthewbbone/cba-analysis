## ARTICLE XI - SENIORITY

1. Seniority by classification shall mean an employee's length of service within a particular job title and grade within a particular School or administrative unit, a list of which is attached as Appendix A.

2. University-wide seniority shall mean an employee's length of service within the bargaining unit covered by this Agreement.

3. Classification seniority in their most recent position only with immediate ability to do the available work shall prevail in laying off employees and in recalling employees from layoff.

4. Any employee displaced from his or her job title shall be permitted to transfer to any vacant position the University is seeking to fill in the same grade and within the same School or administrative unit as defined above, provided he/she has the ability to immediately perform the available work.

5. Any employee displaced from his or her job title shall be permitted to exercise seniority (computed on a University-wide basis) by bumping a less senior employee in a lower rated job title within the same School or administrative unit as defined above, provided he/she has the ability to immediately perform the available work.

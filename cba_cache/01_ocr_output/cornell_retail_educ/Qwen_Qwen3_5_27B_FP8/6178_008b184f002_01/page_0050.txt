## ARTICLE 50
## FULLY BARGAINED PROVISIONS

50-1. This Agreement represents and incorporates the complete and final understanding and settlement by the parties on all bargainable issues for the life of this Agreement, and neither party shall be required to negotiate on any subject unless they mutually agree to do so.

## ARTICLE 51
## MISCELLANEOUS

51-1. There shall be no reprisals of any kind taken by the Board, its servants, agents, or employees against any teacher or member of the JCEA by reason of membership in the JCEA or participation of its activities or by reason of his-her employment and/or his-her school-connected activities.

51-2. The President of the JCEA shall be permitted to attend to Association affairs to the same degree as was true prior to the 1978-80 school year, and shall continue to be granted adequate office facilities.

51-3. The Association agrees that teachers will, if requested, meet with parents during the evening not more than twice during the school year exclusive of the traditional open-house practice. One of said three (3) meetings shall be held after school hours and the other two (2) shall be held in the evening.

## ARTICLE 52
## DURATION

52-1. The provisions of this Agreement shall be effective as of September 1, 1978, and shall remain in full force and effect until August 31, 1980, subject to the right of the Board and Association to negotiate for a modification of this Agreement as provided in Article 3 of said Agreement.

52-2. All present conditions covered by any previous Agreement and those items now in effect as regular employer-employee practice shall remain in effect unless otherwise provided for in the 1978-80 Contract or unless changed hereinafter by mutual consent.

Section 3. All regular part time employees are entitled to holiday pay provided they average twenty (20) hours per week for four (4) weeks immediately preceding the holiday and provided they have six (6) calendar months service with the Company.

Section 4. In a week which includes any of the holidays noted in Section 1 above, the work week shall be composed of thirty-two (32) hours of work and eight (8) hours of holiday time. All work performed in excess of thirty-two (32) hours in such a week shall be compensated at time and one-half.

## ARTICLE IX.
## VACATION PLAN

The Company will grant a paid vacation, as set forth under Section 1, and subject to the conditions under Section 2 of this Article, to eligible employees covered by this Agreement.

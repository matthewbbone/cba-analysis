2. Employees who, as of June 29, 2000, were paid at Step 7 on the salary schedule provided in Appendix A-2 shall, effective June 30, 2000, be paid at a salary rate on the salary schedule provided in Appendix B-3 equal to that which the employee received on the salary schedule provided in Appendix A-2. For the purposes outlined in Section 4 of this Article, said employees shall, upon conversion to the salary schedule provided in Appendix B-3, be provided with a date of next step increase in the next fiscal year which corresponds to the month and day on which the employee received his/her most recent step rate increase.

Employees who, as of June 29, 2000, were paid at Step 6 on the salary schedule provided in Appendix A-2 shall, upon the date of their next step increase, be paid at a salary rate on the salary schedule provided in Appendix B-3 which equals Step 7 of the employee's job group as of June 30, 2000 on the salary schedule provided in Appendix A-2, subject to the provisions of Section 2 of this Article.

4. Employees who, as of June 29, 2000, were paid at Steps 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 on the salary schedule provided in Appendix A-2 shall, effective June 30, 2000, be paid at a step on the salary schedule provided in Appendix B-3 which is next higher to the step which at least equals their June 29, 2000 salary rate on the salary schedule provided in Appendix A-2. The date of next step increase for said employees shall not be changed as a result of this conversion.

E. Employees hired, reinstated or reemployed on or after May 4, 1997, as well as employees transferring into this bargaining unit from a bargaining unit not covered by this Agreement on or after that date, shall be paid in accordance with the salary schedules provided in Appendices B-1 through B-7 of this Agreement.

## Section 12.2

Employees who receive an “unsatisfactory” or “below” rating on their annual EPRS evaluation shall not be eligible to receive the salary increases or bonuses provided in Section 12.1 of this Article nor any step increases. Employees who receive an “unsatisfactory” or “below” rating will have their performance reviewed on a monthly basis in accordance with Article 24A and Supplemental Agreement III of this Agreement and will become eligible for the salary and step increase previously denied effective upon the date of receiving a “satisfactory”, “meets”, or “exceeds” rating.

## Section 12.3

Salary rates and the effective dates of salary increases of full-time employees are set forth in Appendix A-1 through A-2 and Appendices B-1 through B-7 of this Agreement which are attached hereto and hereby made a part of this Agreement.

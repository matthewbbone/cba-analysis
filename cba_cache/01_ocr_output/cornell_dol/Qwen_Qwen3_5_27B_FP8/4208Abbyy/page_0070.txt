**4. Ambulance services**

Covered ambulance services are paid at ninety-five percent (95%) of usual and customary charges.

Varies by plan.

**5. Durable medical equipment**
a. In regions where a network is available, as determined by the service representative

Covered durable medical equipment is paid according to network provider and non-network provider levels, as described in Section 11.C.2 and Section 11.C.3.a.1).

Covered durable medical equipment is paid at eighty (80%) percent of usual and customary charges; limits vary by plan.

b. In regions where a network is not available, as determined by the service representative

Covered durable medical equipment is paid at eighty percent (80%) of usual and customary charges, as described in Section 11.C.3.b).

Covered durable medical equipment is paid at eighty percent (80%) of usual and customary charges; limits vary by plan.

**6. Alternatives to hospitalization**

Covered services and supplies provided by a skilled nursing facility or a hospice agency are paid at one hundred percent (100%) of usual and customary charges.

When approved by the plan, covered services and supplies provided by a skilled nursing facility, a home health care agency, and a hospice agency are paid in full.

**7. Treatment of mental illness**
a. Inpatient treatment coordinated through the network's referral service

Covered services for inpatient treatment of mental illness are paid at ninety-five percent (95%) of allowed charges.

Covered services for inpatient treatment of mental illness are paid in full to thirty (30) days each year if coordinated through the plan.

b. Outpatient treatment coordinated through the network's referral service

Covered services for outpatient treatment of mental illness are paid at a constant eighty percent (80%) of allowed charges.

Covered services for outpatient treatment of mental illness are paid in full to thirty (30) visits each year if coordinated through the plan.

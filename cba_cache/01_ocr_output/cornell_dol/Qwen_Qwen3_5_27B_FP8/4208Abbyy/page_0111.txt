## SECTION 19
## TERMINATION OF COVERAGE

8 A. Life Insurance Coverage

10 Life insurance coverage stops on the date employment terminates.

12 Within thirty-one (31) days after the employee terminates employment, by making application
13 and paying first premium to the Plan's insurer, the employee may convert life insurance coverage
14 to an individual life insurance policy on any regular whole life insurance plan. This individual
15 policy will be issued, without medical examination, at the insurer's regular rates. The amount
16 of life insurance converted cannot exceed the amount in force on the date insurance
17 terminates.

19 If, after an individual conversion policy is issued, benefits under the Life Insurance Plan are
20 continued due to total disability, the individual policy must be surrendered without claim other
21 than the return of paid premiums.

23 If the employee dies within the thirty-one (31)-day conversion period, the conversion amount is
24 payable.

26 An employee who is being transferred and is no longer eligible for coverage under the Life Insurance
27 Plan, but who remains employed by the Company or one of its subsidiaries, also may convert the
28 difference between the amount of life insurance provided by the Life Insurance Plan less the amount
29 provided by the plan for which the employee has become eligible. Application must be made within
30 thirty-one (31) days of the date of transfer.

32 B. Accidental Death and Dismemberment Coverage

34 Accidental death and dismemberment coverage stops on the date employment terminates.

36 C. Medical Coverage

38 Medical coverage for the employee and dependents stops at the end of the calendar month in which
39 the employee terminates employment or the end of the last month required contributions are paid,
40 whichever occurs first. If earlier, a dependent's coverage stops at the end of the month in which he or
41 she no longer qualifies as a dependent.

43 However, coverage may be continued under certain circumstances as specified below. Any required
44 contributions must be paid during these periods for coverage to continue.

46 1. In case of layoff, medical coverage for employees and dependents continues until the employee is
47 covered by any other group medical plan either as an employee or as a dependent, but in no event
48 beyond three (3) months after the date of layoff.

50 2. If the employee dies (other than from an industrial accident), medical coverage continues for
51 eligible dependents until the earlier of twelve (12) months after the employee's death or when
52 the dependents become covered by any other group medical plan.

54 3. If the employee dies from an industrial accident, medical coverage continues for eligible
55 dependents until the earlier of thirty-six (36) months after the employee's death or when the
56 dependents become covered by any other group medical plan.

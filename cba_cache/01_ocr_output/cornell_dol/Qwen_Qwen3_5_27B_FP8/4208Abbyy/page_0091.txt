28 DENTAL PLANS
29 (PREFERRED DENTAL PLAN AND PREPAID DENTAL PLAN)
30
31 A. Covered Services and Supplies
32
33 The following services and supplies are covered under both the Preferred Dental Plan and the Prepaid
34 Dental Plan to the specified limits. The plans pay for covered services only if those services are
35 performed by or under the direction of a licensed dentist or other plan-approved licensed professional.
36 A licensed dentist does not mean a dental mechanic or any other type of dental technician. Coverage
37 is subject to the benefit payment levels, exclusions, and other provisions of each dental plan.
38
39 1. Diagnostic.
40
41 Routine examinations, X-rays, emergency examinations, and examinations by specialists in an
42 American Dental Association recognized specialty.
43
44 Examinations are covered once in a six (6)-month period. Charges to review a proposed
45 treatment plan or for case presentation by the attending dentist are not covered. Complete mouth
46 or panorex X-rays are covered once in a five (5)-year period. Supplementary bitewing X-rays
47 are covered once in a twelve (12)-month period. Study and diagnostic models and decay
48 susceptibility tests are not covered.
49
50 2. Preventive.
51
52 Prophylaxis (cleaning), either a regular prophylaxis or a periodontal prophylaxis, and topical
53 application of fluoride.

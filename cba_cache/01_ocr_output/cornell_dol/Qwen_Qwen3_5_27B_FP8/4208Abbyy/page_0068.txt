b. Mail service prescription drug program

A $10 co-payment applies to each covered generic prescription or refill obtained from the network's mail service prescription drug program. A $30 co-payment applies to each covered brand-name formulary prescription or refill obtained from the network's mail service prescription drug program. A $60 co-payment applies to each covered brand-name non-formulary prescription or refill obtained from the network's mail service prescription drug program. These co-payment amounts provide up to a ninety (90)-day supply.

A mail service prescription drug program may not be available in all coordinated care plans.

If a program is available, a $10 co-payment applies to each covered generic prescription or refill obtained from the network's mail service prescription drug program. A $30 co-payment applies to each covered brand-name formulary prescription or refill obtained from the network's mail service prescription drug program. A $60 co-payment applies to each covered brand-name non-formulary prescription or refill obtained from the network's mail service prescription drug program. These co-payment amounts provide up to a ninety (90)-day supply.

4. Vision examination co-payment

A $15 co-payment applies to each covered eye examination performed by a network provider.

## C. Plan Payment Levels

Plan payment levels are subject to all provisions of the selected plan, including medical review requirements, maximum benefits, coordination of benefits, exclusions, and definitions.

After satisfaction of the deductible and co-payment requirements, the plans pay for covered medical services and supplies according to the following chart. (Payment levels for the coordinated care plans may vary from the schedule as required by applicable state law.)

Traditional Medical Plan Coordinated Care Plans

1. Network hospitals

Covered services are paid in full when received from a network hospital that meets patient safety standards.

Covered services are paid at ninety-five percent (95%) of the allowed charge when received from a network hospital that does not meet patient safety standards.

Covered services received from a network hospital are paid in full.

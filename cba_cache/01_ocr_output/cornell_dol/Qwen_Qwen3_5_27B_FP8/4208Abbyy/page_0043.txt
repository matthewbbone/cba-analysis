31 Attachment 7
32 LETTER OF UNDERSTANDING
33 RELATING TO OVERSIGHT OF LABOR-MANAGEMENT COOPERATIVE INITIATIVES
34
35 The parties enter this Letter of Understanding to establish a joint committee to oversee labor management
36 initiatives the parties undertake. These joint initiatives are intended to enhance and develop employees as
37 the Company's key resource.
38
39 The Joint Union-Company Oversight Committee shall consist of up to four (4) persons representing the
40 Company and four (4) persons representing the Union. The Company representatives will be appointed from
41 Business Unit and Human Resources management. The Union representatives will include three (3)
42 employees and the Union's Executive Director or designee. Each party shall appoint a chairperson of its group.
43
44 The Joint Oversight Committee may oversee initiatives:
45
46 • Monitoring and exploring developments in the areas of education and training, skill utilization and
47 application, and career development as those link to emerging technologies including the Ed Wells
48 Initiative and Technical Principal Program.
49
50 • Monitoring developments in the areas of use of compensatory time off, child and elder care, Drug
51 and Alcohol Free Workplace Program, and the Employee Assistance Program.
52
53 • Exploring alternate forms of compensation and delivery methods, salary planning process, market
54 relationships and compensation philosophy.
55
56 • Discussion groups on topics of mutual interest.

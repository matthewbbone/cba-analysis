17 SECTION 3
18 HOW TO ENROLL
19
20 A. Initial Enrollment
21
22 The retired employee and eligible dependents automatically will be enrolled at the time the
23 retired employee becomes eligible, provided the retired employee pays any required contributions. The
24 retired employee and dependents are enrolled in the same plan as immediately before retirement, if
25 applicable.
26
27 A retired employee who has been enrolled in a health maintenance organization (HMO) or
28 coordinated care plan may elect to change to the Traditional Medical Plan by calling the Boeing
29 Service Center within thirty-one (31) days of the date the employee retires. The Company will
30 supply enrollment instructions at the time of retirement.
31
32 All family members, including the retired employee, must be enrolled in the same medical plan.
33
34 B. Spouse Coverage
35
36 Each retired employee with a spouse must provide information regarding coverage available through
37 another employer to determine whether special contributions are required to enroll the spouse. If the
38 retired employee does not authorize a required contribution, the spouse will not be enrolled for
39 medical coverage. The retired employee will not be able to enroll the spouse until the date the spouse
40 loses the option to be covered under the other employer-sponsored medical plan.
41
42 The Company will require periodic verification of data.
43
44 C. Special Enrollment
45
46 If a retired employee declined enrollment for himself/herself or dependents in the Retiree
47 Medical Plan because of other employer-sponsored health care coverage (such as through a spouse's
48 employer), the retired employee may be able to enroll himself/herself and eligible dependents in the
49 Company-sponsored Retiree Medical Plan at a later date as long as enrollment is within sixty (60) days
50 after other coverage ends.
51
52 If a retired employee declined enrollment for himself/herself or dependents when first eligible and the
53 retired employee's or dependent's other health care coverage was through continuation coverage from
54 a previous employer (coverage mandated by the Consolidated Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of
55 1985, or COBRA), the retired employee or dependent must exhaust his or her COBRA coverage to
56 be eligible for the special enrollment period.

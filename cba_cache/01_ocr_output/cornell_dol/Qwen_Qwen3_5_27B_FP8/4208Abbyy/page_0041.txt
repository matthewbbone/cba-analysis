31 Attachment 3
32 LETTER OF UNDERSTANDING
33 RELATING TO DRUG AND ALCOHOL FREE WORKPLACE PROGRAM
34
35 The Company and the Union enter this Letter of Understanding to address the serious societal problem
36 of drug and alcohol abuse. The Company and the Union affirm their joint objective to achieve a drug and
37 alcohol free workplace while complying with applicable government laws and regulations. To that end, the
38 parties agree to a drug and alcohol free workplace program as described in PRO-388.
39
40 The Company will continue its drug and alcohol awareness program designed to keep employees informed
41 of the drug and alcohol free workplace program, including opportunities for professional assistance
42 through the EAP, the dangers of drug and alcohol abuse, and drug and alcohol testing.
43
44 The Company will maintain a drug and alcohol free workplace training program for its managers, medical
45 professionals, and other selected employees. Union selected individuals, including but not limited to the
46 Union's Executive Board, Council Representatives, and staff members, will be invited to participate in
47 training. Once a year the Union will provide the Company with a list of those persons to be trained.
48
49 The Company will implement a drug and alcohol testing program designed to deter abuse and to provide
50 a means for early identification, referral for treatment, and rehabilitation of employees with abuse
51 problems. The Company will at all times comply with its policy and procedures and with applicable
52 government laws and regulations designed to safeguard the accuracy and reliability of drug and alcohol
53 testing and to protect the confidentiality of those tested.

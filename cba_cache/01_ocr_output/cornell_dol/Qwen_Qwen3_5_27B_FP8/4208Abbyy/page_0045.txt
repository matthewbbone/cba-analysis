20 **LETTER OF UNDERSTANDING**
21 **RELATING TO VIRTUAL OFFICE**
22
23 The parties enter into this Letter of Understanding as a result of the implementation of the Virtual Office Program.
24 Following is a summary of the general provisions of this Program as they apply to SPEEA-represented employees.
25
26 Aspects of the Virtual Office have proven to be a viable work option that, when appropriately applied,
27 benefit both the Company and the individual. The Virtual Office provides a balance between the tasks
28 that are the responsibility of each individual and the requirements of each team and group.
29
30 The Virtual Office is a cooperative agreement between the manager and the employee, not an entitlement,
31 and is based on: (1) the needs of the job assignment, work group and the Company, and (2) the
32 employee's past and present levels of performance and defined personal characteristics. Specifications
33 related to cost, number of days per week, equipment, and participation criteria are not specified in Boeing
34 policy. Each organization has the latitude to utilize and tailor the application of Virtual Office policy to
35 best meet its needs according to requirements, skills, and responsibilities.
36
37 The employee's duties, obligations, responsibilities and conditions of employment with the Company
38 remain unchanged. Employees remain obligated to comply with all Company rules, policies, practices and
39 instructions. Participation in the Virtual Office Program 'Work at Home' element is entirely voluntary
40 and may be terminated by the employee, his or her manager, or the Company at any time.
41
42 The detailed terms and conditions of this Program are covered in the Virtual Office Program procedure,
43 PRO-497, which is subject to change at the Company's discretion. The parties intend that union-
44 represented employees be eligible to participate in the Program to the same extent as non-union employees.
45
46 Disputes concerning the content of this Letter of Understanding shall not be subject to the grievance and
47 arbitration procedure of Article 3. Nothing in this Letter waives any rights reserved in Article 2.

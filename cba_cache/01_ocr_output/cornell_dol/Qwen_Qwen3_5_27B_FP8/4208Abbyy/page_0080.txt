## E. Special Conditions

Covered *medical services and supplies* described in Section 11.D also are provided for the following special conditions.

1. Congenital abnormalities and hereditary complications.

Benefits are provided for *medically necessary services and supplies* required for the treatment of congenital abnormalities and hereditary complications. This coverage applies to newborn children as well as to all other persons covered under the Plan.

2. Cosmetic surgery.

Benefits are provided for cosmetic surgery only if the surgery is for prompt repair of an accidental injury.

4 8.1(b) Job Classification and SMC of Record Shall Prevail. Notwithstanding any challenges
5 concerning job classification and SMC by individual employees or the Union under Article 3 or
6 Article 22, employee reassignments or layoffs effected under the provisions of this Article shall be
7 based upon each employee's job classification and SMC of record at the time of such action.
8 Individual employee or Union contentions that a re-assignment or layoff is inappropriate, inasmuch
9 as the employee's assigned job classification and SMC prior to or at the time of such re-assignment or
10 layoff is or was alleged to be inappropriate, are specifically excluded from the grievance procedure
11 described in Article 3, and shall afford no basis for any claim on the part of the individual employee
12 or Union that such re-assignment or layoff should be voided or set aside.

14 Section 8.2 Procedure Relating to the Filling of Positions.
15
16 8.2(a) The parties are agreed that it is in their mutual interest to assure that favorable promotional
17 and retention consideration is granted to those individuals who are best able to maintain or improve
18 the efficiency of the Company, further its progress and contribute to the successful accomplishment
19 of current and future business. Accordingly, in the filling of positions, particular attention will be
20 given to the development, advancement and retention of the existing workforce.
21
22 8.2(b) Re-assignments and transfers of the following kinds may be made by the Company without
23 regard to the provisions of 8.2(e). Positions so filled shall not be regarded as open positions.
24
25 8.2(b)(1) Re-assignments of surplus employees and surplus individuals from management,
26 engineering, or other salaried payrolls.
27
28 8.2(b)(2) Non-promotional reassignments of non-surplus employees (as, for example, to staff
29 new programs or to avoid surpluses).
30
31 8.2(b)(3) Return of employees from layoff status or from leaves of absence.
32
33 8.2(b)(4) Transfers into the bargaining unit of individuals who at some previous time were
34 assigned to Job Classification and Skills Management Codes currently within the bargaining
35 unit.
36
37 8.2(c) An "in-place" promotion is the promotion of an employee to a higher level within the
38 same Job Classification and Skills Management Code. This promotion results from expansion
39 of the employee's own work assignment and is not for the purpose of filling a position vacated
40 by another employee. Such an in-place promotion does not constitute the filling of a position
41 within the meaning of 8.2, and the Company may make such in-place promotions without
42 limitations.
43
44 8.2(d) All vacant positions other than those filled as described in 8.2(b) and 8.2(c) shall be designated
45 as open positions.
46
47 8.2(e) The Company will seek candidates from within the existing workforce for all positions that are
48 designated by the Company as open positions. Employees on the active payroll who have been
49 declared surplus and/or who have been previously downgraded shall have priority rights to open
50 positions as described in 8.2(e)(1) and 8.2(e)(2), respectively. For open positions remaining after the
51 provisions of 8.2(e)(1) and 8.2(e)(2) have been met, other candidates shall receive consideration as
52 described in 8.2(e)(3).
53
54 8.2(e)(1) If an open position occurs for a Job Classification and Skills Management Code in
55 which layoffs are authorized, an employee already assigned to that Skills Management Code shall
56 be selected for the open position.

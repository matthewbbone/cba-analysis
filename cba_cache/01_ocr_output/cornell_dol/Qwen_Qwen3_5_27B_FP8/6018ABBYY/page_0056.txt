Charges in excess of reasonable and customary charges, which
will be applied to all (out of PPO network) outpatient services as

deductible or annual co-pay maximum requirements of the

All determinations made by the URO affect only the level of reim-
busement for medical services under the **Traditional** Medical
Plan. The decision to enter a hospital, to stay a longer or shorter
period of time than determined by the URO, to have or not have a
second surgical opinion, or to have or not have surgery or other
medical services is solely the responsibility of the covered person
or their legal guardian.

CLASSIFICATIONS

## QUALIFYING PERIOD
## FOR GENERAL FIELD OPERATOR

**Distribution**

General Fitter: 180 days with an optional 90 day extension based on evaluations if needed.

Maintenance Fitter: First must qualify as a General Fitter in Distribution, 180 days. Once qualified as a General Fitter, employee would have 180 days with an optional 90 day extension based on evaluations if needed to qualify as a General Fitter in Field Service.

**Field Service**

General Service Technician: 180 days with an optional 90 day extension based on evaluations if needed.

Service Technician: First must qualify as a General Service Technician in Field Service, 180 days. Once qualified as a General Service Technician, employee would have 180 days with an optional 90 day extension based on evaluations if needed to qualify as a General Field Operator in Distribution.

**NOTE:** A Maintenance Fitter or a Service Technician who fails to qualify for General in their own area would not be eligible for advancement to General Field Operator.

The 90 day extension for qualifying as a General Field Operator must be mutually agreed upon by the Company and the Union.

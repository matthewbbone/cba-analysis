**Assigning overall ratings:**

In order to achieve an overall rating, both Quality and Production numbers must be at a level consistent with that rating. If the employee has a lower rating in either Quality or Production, the overall rating will reflect the lower score.

Exception: if the lower rating actual results are in the top half of the performance range, employee will be given the higher rating overall. Example: 92% Quality and 11.6 call value will result in overall EE (first year).

Example: 92% Quality and 11.1 call value will result in overall ME.

It is agreed that the performance standards negotiated shall remain in effect throughout the term of the contract. However, during the four months after implementation of the new system, the parties agree that productivity standards for performance pay purposes will not be used and that the quality standards will be used exclusively.

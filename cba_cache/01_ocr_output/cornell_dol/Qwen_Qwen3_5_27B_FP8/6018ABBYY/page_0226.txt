## Warehouse Management System

The parties recognize that the Company needs to have qualified Stock employees to enter data into the Warehouse Management System (WAMS). Therefore, the parties agree that the Company will train employees in the General Storekeeper and Storekeeper classifications to perform this work when assigned by the Company in addition to supervision and salaried employees in the Company.

## ARTICLE XI

Field Service/Distribution pursuant to the Collective Bargaining Agreement also shall have the right to terminate such assignment during the first ninety (90) days of the qualifying period (forty-five (45) days in Field Service and forty-five (45) days in Distribution) and will be returned to their former classification and department. Nonunion employees and Non-Local #80 bargaining unit members who transfer into Local #80 Distribution/Field Service will be treated the same as new hires.

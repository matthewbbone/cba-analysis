## ARTICLE XXIV - ANNUAL PHYSICAL EXAMINATIONS

251. When an employee is notified of the need to complete his/her annual physical
examination, he/she may select from the Department of Medicine a
Permanente Medical Group, Inc. physician of his/her choice; however, if the
employee fails to secure an appointment for his/her physical examination
within thirty (30) days following notification, the Employer may assign a
Permanente Medical Group, Inc. physician for the examination.

## ARTICLE XXV - UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION

252. The Employer will cause employees to be covered by unemployment
insurance and disability compensation in accordance with the terms of the
California Unemployment Insurance Code.

253. The above coverage may be adjusted during the life of this Agreement in the
event future legislation is enacted that is applicable to non-profit hospitals.

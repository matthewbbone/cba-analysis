## Section 7 - Promotions, Transfers and Seniority

45. In filling any vacancy, all qualified employees shall be preferred over outside applicants. Between existing employees, bargaining unit seniority shall govern provided merit and ability are adequate. However, for those positions that require the directing and checking of others, the Employer shall have discretion in filling the position by selecting from among the three (3) most senior qualified applicants within the department. In the event there are less than three (3) applicants, the Employer shall have the option to consider applicants from the facility or region, in that order.

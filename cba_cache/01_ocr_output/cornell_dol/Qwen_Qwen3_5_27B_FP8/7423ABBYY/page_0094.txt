# MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

Between

**VERIZON NORTH INC. (NORTH CENTRAL REGION)**

And

**INTERNATIONAL BROTHERHOOD OF ELECTRICAL WORKERS, LOCAL 986**

## FLEX TIME

Effective January 1, 1997, Verizon North Inc. (North Central Region) and the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, Local 986 agree to the following provision regarding flexible work time.

When mutually agreed between supervisor and employee(s), the employee(s) may work a flexible work week schedule, without overtime until after 40 hours are worked. This would be without loss of any other applicable premium. The request for a flexible work schedule may be initiated by either party.

This Memorandum of Agreement shall continue in force year to calendar year unless terminated by either party with a thirty (30) day written notice served by either party.

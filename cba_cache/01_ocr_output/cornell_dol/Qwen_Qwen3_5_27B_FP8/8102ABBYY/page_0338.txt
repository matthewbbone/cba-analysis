National Memorandum

January 9, 2003

Mr. Robert G. Richhart, Administrative Assistant to Vice President, CWA

Dear Mr. Richhart:

Re: Funds for the Alliance/ETOP Distribution Program (FAED) Administration

This will confirm our agreement that for the life of the 2003 Memorandum of Understanding, Lucent agrees to establish an account with the FAED vendor (currently The Alliance) to pre-fund FAED expenditures on behalf of CWA represented Lucent employees. The initial pre-funded amount will be $100,000. Additional funding will be deposited as needed.

Sincerely,

Workforce Relations Vice President

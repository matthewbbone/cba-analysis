**Eligible Vacation Days For Employee Leaving Company**

| :--- | :---: |

| September | 7 | 11 | 16 | 19 |
| October | 8 | 13 | 17 | 21 |
| November | 9 | 14 | 19 | 23 |
| December | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |

(c) An Employee granted vacation or allowance in lieu thereof as provided in Paragraph 4(a) shall also be granted vacation or allowance in lieu thereof, as applicable, for any vacation rescheduled or carried over from the previous calendar year in accordance with Paragraph 2(e), 2(f) or 2(g), respectively.

(d) In the event an Employee dies before taking all the vacation to which eligible as provided in this Article, an allowance in lieu of the vacation not taken will be paid to the Employee's beneficiary, or to the Employee's estate if no beneficiary is designated.

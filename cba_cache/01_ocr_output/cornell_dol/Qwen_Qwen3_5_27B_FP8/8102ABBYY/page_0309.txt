Note 2: An absence for Emergency Service does not affect an employee's right or eligibility with respect to Military Training Duty, Initial Active Duty for Training, or Active Duty for Military Service. If the local emergency situation exceeds 20 scheduled workdays, pay treatment for additional time must be approved by the Lucent Pension Plan Administrator.

## NEW ORLEANS ZONE

### Eastern Boundary

Beginning at the intersection of St. Ann and the Mississippi River, south along the western bank of the Mississippi River to Pontchartrain Expressway.

### Southern Boundary

Beginning at the intersection of the Pontchartrain Expressway and the western bank of the Mississippi River, west along the Pontchartrain Expressway to Annunciation St., then south on Annunciation St. to Melpomene Ave., then northwest on Melpomene Ave. to Claiborne Ave.

### Western Boundary

Beginning at the intersection of Melpomene Ave. and Claiborne Ave., northeast on Claiborne Ave. to Iberville.

### Northern Boundary

Beginning at the intersection of Claiborne Ave. and Iberville, southeast on Iberville to Rampart St., then northeast on Rampart St. to St. Ann, then southeast on St. Ann to the Mississippi River.

## NEW YORK ZONE

The area within the boundaries of the Boroughs of Brooklyn, Bronx, Manhattan and Queens.

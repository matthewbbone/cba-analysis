## ARTICLE 11

Non-teaching Duties

11-1 The Board and the Association acknowledge that a teacher's primary responsibility is to teach, and that his/her ener-gies should be utilized to this end. Therefore, the Board agrees to make every effort to acquire sufficient educational assistants and part-time clerical assistants for the purpose of relieving teachers of non-teaching duties.

11-2 Teachers will not be required to drive pupils to activi-ties which take place away from the school building.

11-3 The Association and the District mutually encourage teacher attendance at educationally-significant meetings scheduled outside of the regular workday.

# Jeffco Schools: JCEA Negotiated Agreement

## APPENDIX 4

### COMPENSATION AGREEMENT 2003-2004

The current salary schedule will be continued for four (4) years from September 1, 2003 through August 31, 2007, with the following exceptions and conditions for the 2003-2004 school year.

1.  Cost of Living adjustment 1.2% effective September 1, 2003.
2.  Steps will be paid effective September 1, 2003, as per normal practice.
3.  Educational levels will continue to be paid as per normal practice.
4.  Insurance - The District will establish a cafeteria benefits plan with the District paying $479.00 toward the plan for the 2003-2004 school year. Employees who opt out of all applicable insurance coverages will receive $160.00 per month. The District Insurance Committee will review the various plan designs and make recommendations to the District and employee groups. The amount the District will contribute toward the insurance benefits programs will be agreed to annually as part of the compensation package during negotiations.
5.  Article 18 - Class Size: For the 2003-2004 school year, the District will maintain the current class size ratios and ranges. The District is committed to maintain funding for Elementary Specials per Article 18 and will fund $1.3 million for class size relief at the elementary level.
6.  Additional Performance Pay Schedule: Increases to the additional performance pay schedule will be implemented as per the normal practice with the District effective September 1 of the 2003-2004 school year.
7.  If the District achieves the additional $9 million Performance Promise dollars, each teacher will receive a 1% additional, one-time, bonus based upon their placement on the 2003-2004 salary schedule.

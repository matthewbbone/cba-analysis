**Section 6.7 – Steward Program**

1. The Union will assign a qualified steward(s) to oversee each Agency facility where bargaining unit Employees are present.

2. To the extent possible, Union Stewards will be assigned representational duties at their duty location. When qualified stewards are not available at one location, the Union may designate a steward or representative from another location to oversee employment matters at the facility experiencing the steward vacancy.

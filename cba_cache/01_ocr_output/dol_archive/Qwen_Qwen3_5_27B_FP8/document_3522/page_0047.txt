## ARTICLE 14 – FURLOUGH AND REDUCTION IN FORCE (RIF)

### Section 14.1 – Furlough

1. Furloughs of thirty (30) days or less will be conducted IAW NGB TPR 715.

2. Furloughs in excess of 30 calendar days (22 workdays) are considered reductions-in-force and will be conducted IAW TPR 300 (351).

### Section 14.2 – Reduction-In-Force (RIF)

The Employer shall notify the Union, as early as possible, of any planned RIF to present its views and recommendation, and must be processed IAW TPR 300 (351).

Agreement Between the Adjutant General of Michigan and the Laborers International Union of North America (LIUNA)
